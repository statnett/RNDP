# How to Get Access to the Research and Development Platform (RNDP)

Please email [Kjell Petter Myhren](mailto:kjell.myhren@statnett.no) in Research and Development (R&D), describing your research project and its needs. If your project is looking for a platform on which to conduct research, RNDP may be the right solution.

Each user must have either a Statnett Entra Id, or a Microsoft Entra ID work account from an organization collaborating with Statnett as a partner.

Microsoft Entra ID is Microsoft's service for managing an organization's user accounts and sign-ins. An Entra ID work account is the account your organization provides for work services, often the same account you use for Microsoft 365 or Teams. It is not a personal Microsoft account. Partner accounts must be set up for federated access to RNDP so that users can sign in with their existing organization's credentials.

General Requirements:

As a prospective platform user, you also need to consider which data your project needs to access.

At the time the source page was written, the platform stores/offers K3 graded production data. Your project will need approval from the data owner to use such data. Depending on the type of data and research involved, GDPR approval may also be required.
